Metro Empire

Build the metro and railway network of real Japanese cities on maps drawn from OpenStreetMap data. Open new sections, run trains, keep them in good repair and grow your income — then expand to the next city.

  • Tokyo with 55 real lines and 544 stations, plus Osaka, Nagoya, Yokohama, Kyoto and Kobe
  • Real landmarks that boost the income of their city
  • Your network keeps earning for a while when you are away
  • Available in 14 languages
  • Free to play, supported by optional and occasional ads
