1. Who we are

Metro Empire is developed and published by Agehesha, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). The app’s Android package name, com.metromagnat.tokyo, is a technical identifier and does not change. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It describes how the current version of the app actually works.

For ads served through Google AdMob, Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects, under its own privacy policy (see section 3).

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves the following information locally on your device so that you can continue playing:

  • Game progress: built lines and stations, trains and their condition, money, landmarks, badges and opened cities.
  • Settings: interface language and color theme.
  • Timing data used by game mechanics: the time of your last session (to calculate income earned while you were away), daily limits for optional rewarded ads, and the time since the last full-screen ad.
  • A correction value between your device clock and the network time (see section 5).

This data is stored in the app’s private storage (WebView local storage, with a backup copy in Android app preferences). It is not sent to us or to any server operated by us. The interface language is chosen from your device’s language settings on the device itself; this setting is not transmitted anywhere by the game.

Android backup. The app allows Android’s standard backup. If you have turned on backup in your Android settings, Android may include the app’s data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is controlled by you and handled by Google under its own terms; we cannot access it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Metro Empire is free and is supported by ads delivered by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google LLC or, for users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google Ireland Limited. The app shows two kinds of ads:

  • Rewarded ads — only when you choose to watch one, for example to receive an in-game bonus or to double your offline income.
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads — occasionally, during natural pauses in the game, and not more often than once every several minutes of active play.

To deliver ads, limit how often they are shown, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects information from your device. According to Google, this can include:

Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDKPurpose (as described by Google)
Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set IDAdvertising, analytics of ad delivery, fraud prevention
IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate locationAdvertising, fraud prevention
Ad interactions: ads viewed and tapped, app launch and session informationAdvertising, analytics
Diagnostic information: crash logs, ad loading performance, device and operating-system detailsAnalytics, fraud prevention, security

Depending on your consent and settings, ads may be personalized (based on your interests) or non-personalized. Data collected by Google is transmitted encrypted. We do not receive your advertising ID or other personal data from Google; AdMob only provides us with aggregated reports, such as the number of ads shown and the revenue earned.

4. Consent and your ad choices

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform. If you are in a region where consent is required for personalized advertising (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), a consent form is shown when you start the game, before ads are requested. You can accept or refuse personalized ads; the game works the same either way.

Other choices you have at any time:

  • Limit ad personalization through Android’s ad privacy settings (Ad topics, App-suggested ads, Ad measurement), where your device supports them.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (on most devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). After deletion, apps cannot use the advertising ID for personalized ads.
  • Change or withdraw your consent: open the game’s menu → Settings → Privacy settings. This opens Google’s official consent form, where you can change your choice at any time. The button is shown where Google’s User Messaging Platform requires this option (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom); it needs an internet connection. If you do not see the button, no consent choice is required for your region. You can also contact us for help.
  • Play offline: without an internet connection no ads are loaded and the game remains playable.

5. Network time check

To prevent changing the device clock from affecting game mechanics, the app periodically sends a simple request to https://www.google.com/generate_204 and reads only the current date and time from the response. The request contains no game data and no identifiers created by the game. As with any internet request, Google’s server receives standard connection information, such as your IP address and basic request headers; this is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Android permissions

  • Internet access and network state — to load ads and check the time.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so that ads can be delivered as described in section 3.
  • Android ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS) — added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. On devices that support Android’s Privacy Sandbox, the SDK may use these system APIs for ad selection and measurement. You can control them in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads → Ad privacy, where available).
  • Background work (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE) — added by the Google SDK’s libraries for internal scheduling of ad-related tasks.

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or phone calls.

7. How data is shared

We do not sell personal data and we do not share your data with anyone, because we do not collect it on our own servers. Data collected directly by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and by the network time check is processed by Google as described above. We may disclose information only if required by law, and only to the extent we actually hold it.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device is kept until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policies. See How Google retains data.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you, and you can ask us to delete them.

9. Security

Game data stays in the app’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Network requests made by the app use encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the amount of data involved to a minimum.

10. Children

Metro Empire is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for any personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or under the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, most requests are best handled by the controls on your device described above or directly with Google for ad-related data, through your Google account. We will gladly help with any request — write to us.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains new features or uses a new service. The current version is always available at this address, with the date of the last update shown at the top. Significant changes will be reflected here before they take effect in the app.

13. Contact

For any question about this policy or your data, contact Agehesha at darkpwaoff@gmail.com. Please write “Privacy” in the subject line.
